How It Works
Tap this icon (top-left of the map) to enter Background mode.
A sidebar of drawing tools opens on the left: Pen, Paintbrush, Fill, Erase, Rectangle, Line, Polygon, Tree/Bush and Flower stamps, and Text. The options for the current tool (colour, width, fill…) sit in a bar along the top of the map. Each new shape is selected as soon as you finish it, so you can tweak it straight away or drag its corner/edge handles to resize it; use Select to move, recolour, resize, restack or delete anything you've drawn. Snap makes new corners and moved shapes click onto nearby shapes; Undo steps back up to 5 changes.
Under Scale, switch on the 1 m grid and enter your plot's real width and length to see a dashed outline of its true size while you draw (1 map unit = 1 cm) — these guides only show in Background mode. Two fingers or the mouse wheel pan and zoom. Tap "Done" when you're happy with the layout.
Back on the main map, tap "+ Add Bed" and pick a shape. Rectangle: drag on the map to size the bed (a plain tap drops a default-size one). Polygon: tap the map to place each corner of the bed. Close the shape by tapping the first corner again, or tap "✓ Finish" (Enter); "✕ Cancel" (Esc) abandons it. Drag a bed's middle to move it — it snaps to nearby beds — or drag any corner dot to reshape it.
Tap this icon (top-right of a card) to record what's planted, key dates, what's coming next, and any notes.
Inside a bed's popup, tap its color to pick from 5 simple presets, and tap the small pencil next to its name to rename it.
The Priorities tab lists everything that needs seeding, planting, or harvesting soon.
The Plant Guide has details for 100+ vegetables, flowers, and herbs — including spacing, companions, and a calendar view.
